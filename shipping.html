How we prepare and deliver your order.

How ordering works

When you place an order, our team reviews it and confirms the details with you by phone or email. Once confirmed, we prepare your items for delivery.

Costs and timing

Shipping costs and estimated delivery times are shown at checkout before you confirm your order, so you always know what to expect. If something causes a delay, we will tell you as early as we can.

Payment on delivery

Orders are paid when they arrive. Please have the agreed amount ready. The order reference you receive at checkout helps us find your order quickly.
