Warranty

Every product lists its warranty details, so you can buy with confidence.

What your warranty covers

Warranty coverage depends on the product and the manufacturer. Each product page shows the warranty information we have for that item. Warranty terms are set by the manufacturer and may cover defects in materials and workmanship, but not accidental damage or normal wear.

Making a claim

Keep your order reference as proof of purchase. If a product develops a fault, contact us with your reference and a description of the problem, and we will guide you through the next steps.
