Returns

Changed your mind, or received something that is not right? Here is how to ask for help.

Contacting us first

If a product is damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible after delivery. Include your order reference and a short description of the problem, and if possible a photo.

Return conditions

Items should be unused and returned in their original packaging, with all accessories included. Our team will explain the steps and confirm whether a return or exchange is possible.

Refunds

When a return is approved, we will tell you how and when your refund will be processed, and by which method.
